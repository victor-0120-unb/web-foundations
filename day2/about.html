About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple tool for capturing and organizing important ideas and reminders. It helps users quickly write down notes so they can keep track of information they do not want to forget.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open QuickNotes and go to the note area.
  2. Type your idea or reminder into the note field.
  3. Click the Add Note button to save your note.
  4. Review your saved notes in the notes list.

Features

  • Quickly create new notes.
  • Keep important reminders in one place.
  • View a list of saved notes.
  • Simple and easy-to-use interface.

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Ctrl + N Create a new note
